Mousy for macOS

Mousy turns your head movements into pointer control: turn your head to point, rest to click. Mousy runs entirely on your Mac, and this build makes no network requests at all.

What you need

  • macOS 15 or later.
  • Headphones with head-tracking sensors: AirPods Pro (any generation), AirPods Max, AirPods (3rd generation or later), or Beats Fit Pro.
  • Accessibility permission, so Mousy may move the pointer.

Set up

  1. Download and unzip, then move Mousy.app into /Applications.
  2. Open Mousy. It is ad-hoc signed, not Developer ID signed or notarized, so macOS may refuse the first launch. If it does, open System Settings > Privacy & Security after the attempt; a blocked app can usually be allowed from there.
  3. When macOS asks, allow Mousy under System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility.
  4. Put your AirPods in and follow the in-app tour.

If you replace Mousy.app with a newer build later, macOS treats it as a new app: remove the old Mousy entry under Accessibility and grant it again.

Honest limits

  • This is an early build. It passed its automated Mac checks (51 tests, verified launch), but head tracking has not yet been exercised with real AirPods on a physical Mac.
  • Update checks are compiled off; new versions are shared here when they exist.
