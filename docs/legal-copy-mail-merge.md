## Suggested Terms or Acceptable Use paragraph

Mail merge and the Spam Act. When you use mail merge to send commercial electronic messages, you must comply with the Spam Act 2003 (Cth). In short: you need the consent of each person you send to (express consent, or consent that can reasonably be inferred from an existing business relationship); each message must clearly identify you as the sender and include your contact details; and each commercial message must include a functional unsubscribe facility, with unsubscribe requests honoured within 5 business days. Multies asks you to confirm consent before every merge, adds an unsubscribe link to merges marked as marketing, skips people who have unsubscribed from your workspace, and limits how many people a merge can reach each day (Free 50, Pro 500, Business 2,000). You remain responsible for the lists you upload and for the content you send. Messages that are purely factual (for example an invoice or a receipt for a purchase already made) are generally not commercial messages, but if they also promote goods or services the Act applies.

## Privacy note (for the privacy policy, mail merge section)

When you run a mail merge, the names, email addresses and field values in your list are used only to make each person's PDF and send their email. The PDFs are stored for up to 3 days so sends can be retried, then deleted. Unsubscribe requests are kept so we can keep honouring them.

## Needs a lawyer's eye

The wording above is a plain summary, not legal advice. Before launch, have it checked against the current Spam Act 2003 and the ACMA guidance, and confirm the 5 business day unsubscribe window and the inferred consent wording.
