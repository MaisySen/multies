# Multies brand

## The logo
The mark is concept A3, chosen by the owner on 24 October 2026: a chunky rounded M with an orange spark above it. The M stands for the many tools and people working as one; the spark is the moment the work moves. The wordmark is Inter SemiBold (SIL OFL 1.1), outlined to paths.

Every asset is generated by `node brand/build.mjs` from the geometry in that file. Do not edit the PNGs by hand. `src/web/components/logo.tsx` draws the same geometry for the web app.

## Files
- `brand/logo-light.svg`, `logo-dark.svg`, `logo-mono.svg`: the horizontal lockup
- `brand/logo-mark.svg`, `logo-mark-dark.svg`, `logo-mark-mono.svg`: the mark alone
- `brand/logo-mark-tile.svg`: the mark on the app icon tile
- `brand/web/`: favicons (svg, ico, 16, 32, 48), apple touch icon, manifest icons (192, 512, maskable), Open Graph card, email header PNG
- `brand/mobile/`: iOS icon, Android adaptive foreground, background and monochrome, splash icon, notification icon

## Colours
- Ink `#1D1D1F` for the M and wordmark on light backgrounds, white on dark
- Spark `#FF6A1A`, used only in the logo spark, never as a UI colour
- Tile `#1D1D1F` for app icons and favicons
- The UI accent stays `#0071E3` (see `brand/tokens.json`)

## Clear space and size
- Keep clear space around the lockup equal to the height of the spark on every side.
- Minimum sizes: lockup 80 px wide on screen, mark alone 16 px. At 32 px and below use the favicon cut (heavier M, larger spark), which the build already produces.

## Do not
- Recolour the M or the spark, add gradients, shadows or outlines
- Stretch, rotate or rearrange the mark and wordmark
- Put the colour lockup on a busy photo; use the mono version instead
- Use the old envelope M, which is retired
