# Multies support

Multies is business email on your own domain, with team chat, tasks and AI agent approvals, for iPhone, Android and the web. It is provided by Fintex Australia Pty Ltd.

## Contact

Email contact@fintex.au. We answer within two business days, Sydney time.

## Common questions

- Signing in: tap Sign in in the app. The Multies sign in page opens in your browser. Use your email and password, or Google, Apple or GitHub.
- No account yet: create one at https://multies.si/signup, or tap Explore with sample data in the app to look around first.
- Notifications: choose which alerts reach your phone in the app under Settings.
- Deleting your account: in the app, Settings, then Delete my account. Or see https://multies.sites.tab.bot/delete-account/.
- Privacy: https://multies.sites.tab.bot/privacy/. Terms: https://multies.sites.tab.bot/terms/.
