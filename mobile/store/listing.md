# Store listing draft (en-AU)

## Name and subtitle
- App name: MyMail
- Subtitle (30 characters): Email for your team and agents
- Google Play short description (80): Your business email, your team and your AI agents, in one inbox.

## Description

MyMail is email on your own domain, made for Australian startups and small businesses.

Read and answer your business mail on the go, with your team and your AI agents working in the
same inbox.

- Inbox, threads, reply and forward, across every mailbox you can open
- Search everything, and read recent mail even with no signal
- Approve what your AI agents want to send. Nothing leaves until you tap Approve, and you can
  edit it first
- Turn any email into a task, then track it on a simple board
- Two step sign in and in-app account deletion

Start free with your own domain at mymail.au.

## App privacy (App Store) and Data safety (Google Play)
- Collected and linked to the person: email address, name, emails or text messages (the mail
  itself), device id for push. Used for app functionality only.
- Not used for tracking. Not sold. Data is encrypted in transit.
- People can delete their account in the app (Settings, Delete account) or on the web.
