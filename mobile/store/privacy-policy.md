# Multies privacy policy

Updated 24 October 2026.

Multies is provided by Fintex Australia Pty Ltd (ABN 60 688 406 108), New South Wales, Australia. Contact: contact@fintex.au. In this policy "we" means Fintex Australia Pty Ltd. This policy covers the Multies app for iPhone and Android and the Multies web app at https://multies.si.

Multies is business email on a domain you own, with team chat, tasks, docs and an approval queue for AI agents that your workspace connects.

## What we collect

- Account details: your name, email address and the sign in method you use (email and password, or Google, Apple or GitHub through our sign in provider). We need these to give you an account. Kept while your account exists.
- Mail: the messages you send and receive in your workspace, their attachments, folders and flags, and delivery records (sender, recipient, time, result). This is the service itself. Kept until you or your workspace delete it, or the account is deleted.
- Workspace content: team chat messages, tasks, comments, docs, time entries, contacts you add, and approvals of messages your AI agents want to send. Needed to run these features. Kept until deleted.
- Identifiers: your user id and workspace id, so the app can show your data and nobody else's. Kept while your account exists.
- Push token: only if you allow notifications and push is switched on for the app. It lets us send alerts to your phone. Removed when you sign out, turn push off or delete your account.
- Security and diagnostic data: IP address, browser or device type for each session, an audit log of changes in your workspace, request logs and error reports. Used to keep accounts safe and to fix errors. Request logs are kept for 30 days. Error reports carry account ids but no message content.

We do not collect your location, contacts from your phone, photos, camera, microphone, health data, browsing history or advertising ID.

## What stays on your device

Your sign in session is kept in the phone's secure storage. A copy of recent mail, tasks and people is cached on the phone so you can read it with no signal. Signing out or deleting your account removes both from the phone. The "Explore with sample data" mode runs on the phone only and sends nothing to us.

## Location

Multies does not use your location.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app registers a push token with our server so we can tell you about new mail, mentions, assignments and approvals. You choose which alerts you get in Settings. We never send marketing pushes.

## AI agents

Multies has no AI of its own in the app. A workspace may connect its own AI agent, or add its own AI provider key in the web app. When it does, the content that workspace chooses to send goes to that provider, on the workspace's own account with them. Messages an agent wants to send wait for a person to approve them.

## Who else is involved

- Cloudflare (hosting, database and the network that serves the app). The database is placed in Cloudflare's Oceania region.
- Resend (delivers the mail you send to other mail providers).
- Auth0 by Okta (sign in, and Google, Apple or GitHub sign in if you choose it).
- Sentry (error reports from our server, with no message content).
- Stripe (payments, only if your workspace buys a paid plan on the web; card details go to Stripe and never reach us).
- Expo, Apple and Google (deliver push notifications, only if they are switched on).

We only use these services to run Multies, and each of them protects the data it handles to the same or a higher standard than this policy. We do not sell your information or share it for advertising. Mail you send goes to the people you address it to, because you asked us to send it.

## Tracking and ads

Multies has no ads and does not track you across apps or websites. The app has no analytics or advertising code.

## Children

Multies is a business app for people 18 and over. It is not made for children and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Your choices and deleting your data

You can delete your account inside the app: Settings, then Delete my account. This deletes your account and every workspace only you belong to, at once. You can also delete it on the web at https://multies.si/delete-account/, or email contact@fintex.au and we will delete what we hold for you within 30 days. Workspace owners can export and delete mail in the web app.

## Security

Data is sent over HTTPS and stored with Cloudflare, with the main database in the Oceania region. Passwords are stored as hashes, API keys are stored hashed, and domain signing keys are encrypted at rest.

## Australian Privacy Principles

We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). You can ask to see or correct what we hold. If you are not happy with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Questions: contact@fintex.au, Fintex Australia Pty Ltd, https://fintex.au.
