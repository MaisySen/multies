# Delete your Multies account

You can delete your Multies account at any time.

## In the app

1. Open Multies and sign in.
2. Go to Settings, then Delete my account.
3. Type DELETE and confirm.

## On the web

Sign in at https://multies.si, open Settings, Account, and choose Delete account.

## By email

Email contact@fintex.au from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We delete what we hold for you within 30 days.

## What is deleted

Your account, your sign in details, your push tokens and every workspace only you belong to, with its mail, chat, tasks and docs, are deleted at once. In a workspace shared with others, your membership is removed and the workspace stays with its other members. Server request logs are removed after 30 days. Copies cached on your phone are removed when the deletion finishes.
