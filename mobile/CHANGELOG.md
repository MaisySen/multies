# Changelog

All notable changes to the Multies mobile app. Versions follow the store version (versionName on
Android, CFBundleShortVersionString on iOS).

## 1.10.15 (OTA, runs on Android versionCode 16 and iOS build 20), 6 Nov 2026

- Calendar in the app: month, week and agenda views, show or hide each calendar, add and edit events, and calendar settings.
- Meetings: read notes, minutes and transcripts right in the app.
- Writing mail: attach from Files, send later, saved replies and send securely.
- Mailbox folders and bulk select: pick many messages and move, archive or mark them at once.
- Shared inbox: assign a thread, leave team comments, and see everything in One inbox.

## 1.10.14 (OTA, runs on Android versionCode 16 and iOS build 20), 4 Nov 2026

- Multies speaks Nepali and Hindi: Today, Inbox, mail, writing mail, Chat, Tasks, More, Search and Settings follow your phone's language, or pick one in Settings, Language. Your account on the web uses the same choice.
- Appearance in Settings: match your phone, always light or always dark, and the Forest green theme from the web app.
- Account in Settings: change your name and password, and use this phone's time zone. Multies sign in accounts open their sign in settings in the app.
- Plan and usage in Settings shows your plan and what your workspace uses. The app has no purchases.
- Get started: the web's checklist is on Today and in Settings, and each step opens its screen in the app.
- Search results for deals and other pages the app has open in the app instead of the web page.

## 1.10.13 (OTA, runs on Android versionCode 16 and iOS build 20), 3 Nov 2026

- A cleaner Today: the colour card now fills the width with Write, My tasks and Search side by side, and nothing overlaps.
- Today shows what matters below the counts: your newest unread mail, what is due today and what is coming up.
- Counts read properly: 15,527 instead of 15527, badges stop at 99+, and unread mail is no longer called today's.
- Clearer badges in dark mode, menu names that fit on small phones, and a short guide on the Security screen before you turn on the lock.

## 1.10.12 (OTA, runs on Android versionCode 16 and iOS build 20), 3 Nov 2026

- A new look: softer cards with depth instead of boxes, rounder corners, true black dark mode, tinted icons and a colour hero on Today with Write, My tasks and Search one tap away.
- Lock Multies: Face ID, Touch ID or your fingerprint, with a 4 or 6 digit PIN when that can't be used. Lock every time or after 1, 5 or 15 minutes, and hide your mail in the app switcher. Settings, Security.
- Invoices are in the app: what is owed and overdue, every invoice with its state, and the customer's link to share.
- Scheduling is in the app: share your booking link in one tap and see the bookings coming up, by day.
- Links are in the app: every short link with its clicks, tap to share.
- A light tap when you switch tabs.

## 1.10.11 (OTA, runs on Android versionCode 16 and iOS build 20), 2 Nov 2026

## 1.9.0 (Android versionCode 11, iOS build 13), 25 October 2026

- Email addresses from inside the app (Settings, Email addresses). Three ways, the same as the web:
  connect an existing mailbox (Gmail, Google Workspace, Outlook.com, Microsoft 365, Yahoo, iCloud,
  Zoho, Fastmail, GMX or any IMAP and SMTP server: pick the provider or let the address pick it,

  server change. Compose with no address now offers "Add an email address" instead of "Add one on
  the web first".
- The app talks to https://multies.si (was the workers.dev host); development builds use
  https://preview.multies.si. Every link the app opens is on multies.si.
- The iOS privacy manifest is checked in the prebuild output (ios/Multies/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy).
- Store answers in the repo match what was entered on 25 Oct 2026: Play data safety now lists Files and docs (chat attachments) and Diagnostics; App Store Diagnostics is Linked to the user, and the privacy manifest (app.json and store/ios/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy) says so too. - Undo send: Send closes Compose and holds the message for 5, 10, 20 or 30 seconds (default 10).
  A bar above the tabs counts down with Undo and Send now; Undo reopens Compose exactly as it was
  (recipients, subject, body, the reply it belongs to). A failed send reopens it too. VoiceOver and
  TalkBack hear the hold once. The choice is in Settings, This phone, and is saved on the phone. No
  server change; the same timing core as the web app (docs/undo-send.md).
